Engineering Architecture

Technical Overview & System Capabilities

Neptune Manufacturing Facility

Neptune Automatic designs and manufactures high-duty automatic boom barriers engineered for extreme vehicular throughput and adverse industrial operating environments across India. Integrating cutting-edge European drive engineering with robust indigenous chassis fabrication, our barrier suite guarantees dependable lane regulation.

From the ultra-fast 0.8s FAAC 620 Rapid hydraulic barrier engineered for congested expressway toll lanes to the heavy-duty 8-meter span Neptune Strong Arm, our products integrate sinusoidal lever linkages, oil-bath planetary gearboxes, absolute encoders, and multi-spring counterbalances for smooth, bounce-free operation.

All Neptune barrier gates seamlessly integrate with FASTag (ETC), long-range UHF RFID, ANPR license plate recognition cameras, safety infrared photocells, and inductive road loops, delivering fully automated, touchless vehicle lane processing.

Technical Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Electromechanical barriers utilize electric motors (typically 24V DC brushless or 230V AC) coupled with mechanical gearboxes and counterbalancing springs. They are cost-effective, energy-efficient, and easy to maintain. Hydraulic barriers utilize an electric motor driving a hydraulic pump with synthetic oil cylinders, providing exceptional endurance under continuous 100% duty cycles, superior wind resistance, and zero mechanical wear on friction components.
Operating speeds vary by model and boom arm length. High-speed toll models such as the FAAC 620 Rapid open in 0.8 seconds. Commercial barriers like the Power Fusion XD and FAAC B614 operate between 1.5s and 2.5s for arms up to 4 meters, while heavy industrial barriers like the Strong Arm (up to 8m) operate in 5.0s to 6.0s to ensure mechanical stability.
Yes. All Neptune barrier models include an ergonomic mechanical manual release key that allows authorized security personnel to manually raise the boom. Furthermore, 24V DC models (such as FAAC B614 and FAAC B680H) feature optional integrated battery backup kits that allow 50 to 100 automatic cycles during power failure.
Absolutely. Every Neptune barrier includes an industrial microprocessor control board equipped with standard Wiegand, RS-485, and dry contact trigger inputs, making them 100% plug-and-play compatible with NPCI-certified FASTag readers, UHF RFID tags, ANPR cameras, and building management systems.
Neptune barrier installations incorporate redundant multi-layer safety features: active infrared safety photocells, inductive sub-surface vehicle ground loops that hold the boom open while a vehicle is present, electronic anti-crush force sensors that immediately reverse boom motion upon detecting resistance, and optional soft foam rubber boom edging with integrated LED strips.
