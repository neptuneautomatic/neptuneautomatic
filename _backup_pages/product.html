Neptune Power Fusion XD Automatic Boom Barrier

Heavy-duty sinusoidal industrial boom barrier engineered for continuous toll and freight operations.

The Neptune Power Fusion XD represents our flagship heavy-duty automatic barrier for industrial applications. Engineered and manufactured at our modern facility, it features a heavy-duty sinusoidal crank-lever linkage that decelerates the boom arm smoothly as it approaches horizontal and vertical stops, virtually eliminating mechanical shock and prolonging gearbox lifespan.

Core Engineering Highlights:
  • Sinusoidal lever crank linkage providing built-in mechanical soft-landing
  • Reinforced boom bracket accommodating round, octagonal, or fence booms up to 6m
  • Dual compression spring counter-balancing system for exact equilibrium
  • High-visibility reflective cabinet styling with anti-corrosion primer coating
