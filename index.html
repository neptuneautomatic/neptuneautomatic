Enterprise Consultation & Technical FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Key answers regarding technical specifications, crash certifications, FASTag integration, civil works, and nationwide AMC maintenance SLAs.

Neptune Automatic boom barriers accommodate arm lengths ranging from 2.5 meters to 8 meters. For high-throughput transit points, toll plazas, and IT campus entrances, our ultra-fast brushless DC (BLDC) models achieve opening speeds as rapid as 0.9 to 1.5 seconds with 100% continuous duty cycle ratings. For long-span industrial logistics gates (up to 8m), the opening cycle is calibrated between 3.0 and 6.0 seconds, accompanied by infrared safety beams, loop detectors, and impact-softening rubber profiles.

Yes. Neptune's high-security automatic bollards and shallow-mount road blockers are engineered and internationally tested according to ASTM F2656, PAS 68, and IWA 14-1 crash standards. Our crash-rated M30 and M50 models are certified to arrest a 7.5-ton hostile truck traveling at 50 km/h and 80 km/h respectively with zero perimeter penetration. They also feature Emergency Fast Operation (EFO), allowing immediate deployment in under 1.0 second during a high-alert scenario.

Neptune Smart PMS comes equipped with NPCI-certified dedicated FASTag overhead RFID readers and optical lane cameras at both entry and exit. FASTag-registered vehicles experience frictionless drive-through access with toll-style automated balance deduction. For non-FASTag visitors, our unattended Pay Stations (APMs) and lane entry dispensers provide dynamic UPI QR codes (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit card tap-to-pay, and ticketless license plate recognition (ANPR) with cloud revenue reconciliation.

Absolutely. All Neptune automation control boards and cloud management modules feature open-architecture design. We support industry-standard protocols including REST APIs, Wiegand 26/34, RS-485, BACnet, and TCP/IP. This ensures seamless interoperability with enterprise access control software (e.g., Honeywell, Lenel, Schneider, Johnson Controls), SAP / Oracle ERP platforms, and corporate HRMS for employee badge and visitor management synchronisation.

Safety is our highest engineering priority. Every barrier and gate installation employs triple redundancy protection: (1) dual buried inductive magnetic loop detectors that lock the barrier in the open position while metal mass is present; (2) active infrared photocell safety beams; and (3) intelligent electronic torque auto-reversing sensors in our BLDC drives that instantly reverse the barrier's direction within milliseconds upon detecting any physical resistance.

Standard automatic boom barriers require a minimal concrete foundation pad (approx. 450 × 450 × 500 mm) with conduit routing and can be mechanically anchored and fully commissioned within 24 to 48 hours. For hydraulic rising bollards and road blockers, excavation depth varies between 350mm (shallow mount) and 1,200mm (deep embedment) with rebar reinforcement and submersible gravel drainage pits; civil work and electrical commissioning typically require 3 to 7 working days per lane.

All Neptune equipment includes a standard comprehensive 1 to 3-year manufacturer warranty covering all drive motors, electronics, and hydraulic power units. For continuous operational readiness, we offer customized Annual Maintenance Contracts (Comprehensive & Non-Comprehensive) backed by our direct nationwide network of factory-trained technicians stationed across 25+ Indian industrial hubs with guaranteed 2 to 4-hour emergency response SLAs and 100% genuine OEM spare parts availability.
