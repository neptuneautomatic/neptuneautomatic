Neptune Power Fusion XD Automatic Boom Barrier

Heavy-duty sinusoidal industrial boom barrier engineered for continuous toll and freight operations.

The Neptune Power Fusion XD represents our flagship heavy-duty automatic barrier for industrial applications. Engineered and manufactured at our modern facility, it features a heavy-duty sinusoidal crank-lever linkage that decelerates the boom arm smoothly as it approaches horizontal and vertical stops, virtually eliminating mechanical shock and prolonging gearbox lifespan.

Core Engineering Highlights:
  • Sinusoidal lever crank linkage providing built-in mechanical soft-landing
  • Reinforced boom bracket accommodating round, octagonal, or fence booms up to 6m
  • Dual compression spring counter-balancing system for exact equilibrium
  • High-visibility reflective cabinet styling with anti-corrosion primer coating
  • Emergency manual release handle inside lockable cabinet door

Engineering R&D Architecture

Kinematics & Design Rationale

Neptune R&D Facility

The Neptune Power Fusion XD represents our flagship heavy-duty automatic barrier for industrial applications. Engineered and manufactured at our modern facility, it features a heavy-duty sinusoidal crank-lever linkage that decelerates the boom arm smoothly as it approaches horizontal and vertical stops, virtually eliminating mechanical shock and prolonging gearbox lifespan.

Equipped with dual compression counterbalance springs, an IP55 cold-rolled steel cabinet, and intelligent electronic obstacle detection, the Power Fusion XD is specifically tailored for continuous freight terminals, toll plazas, and harsh outdoor manufacturing environments across India.

Native support for Wiegand, RS-485, and dry contact interfaces ensures rapid, reliable interoperability with FASTag RFID readers, automatic number plate recognition (ANPR) systems, and factory management controllers.

Industrial Engineering

Key Engineering Subsystems & Technology

01

Sinusoidal Crank-Lever Kinematics

Converts constant rotational motor torque into sinusoidal motion, automatically decelerating the boom arm at both limits without relying solely on software electronic braking.

02

Dual Compression Spring Balance

Precision balanced springs allow effortless manual lifting during power cuts and reduce operating motor current and heat dissipation by over 60%.

03

Heavy Cold-Rolled Steel Cabinet

2.5mm heavy-gauge steel treated with zinc phosphating and electrostatic polyester powder coating to withstand aggressive industrial air, salt, and monsoons.
