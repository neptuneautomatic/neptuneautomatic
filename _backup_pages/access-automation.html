Engineering Architecture

Technical Overview & System Capabilities

Neptune R&D Center

Neptune Automatic designs, fabricates, and integrates premier perimeter automation systems engineered for high vehicular throughput and harsh industrial operating conditions across India. Combining precision European gearmotors and hydraulic power units with heavy structural steel chassis, our product line ensures absolute physical security and frictionless transit.

From high-speed sub-second highway toll barriers (FAAC 620 Rapid) to ASTM F2656 crash-tested anti-ram bollards and 4000 kg industrial gate operators, our solutions integrate sinusoidal crank-levers, absolute encoders, dual loop sensors, and emergency manual release mechanisms.

Every system offers native plug-and-play interoperability with FASTag (ETC), UHF RFID, AI-powered ANPR cameras, safety photocells, and building management systems (BMS).

Continuous 100% duty cycle rating engineered for non-stop industrial operations
European hydraulic and 24V brushless drive technologies with up to 5M MCBF
Certified Hostile Vehicle Mitigation (HVM) ASTM F2656 M50 / PAS 68 anti-ram defense
Integrated micro-processor controllers with Wiegand, RS-485, and dry relay inputs
Emergency manual release keys & optional 24V DC battery backup systems
Full-scale pan-India factory service SLA with direct indigenous spares availability
